Klarity

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

The short version

Klarity does not collect, transmit or share personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party SDK that collects data. The app is free and has no in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

The apps you pick, your rules, your pause history and your streak are stored only on your device, in Klarity’s private storage (on-device storage). They are never uploaded.

Klarity cannot see what you do inside other apps. It only records the moments you choose to open an app or skip it after a pause, when you use the pause flow (including via a Shortcuts automation you set up).

Notifications

If you turn on the evening check-in, Klarity schedules a local notification on your device. No server is involved, and no push token is collected.

Backups you choose to share

The “Back up my rules” feature uses your device’s share sheet. You decide where that text goes. If you share it off the device, that copy is under your control, not ours.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything in Settings > Erase all data, or by deleting the app.

Children

Klarity is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.
