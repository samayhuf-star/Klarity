How does Klarity work?

Klarity inserts a short pause before you open apps you choose. After a one-time setup in Apple’s Shortcuts app, opening a chosen app can open Klarity first, with a brief breathing pause. You then choose to do something else or to open the app. You can also try the pause from the Today tab with the “Open” button, without Shortcuts. Klarity does not use the Screen Time or Family Controls API and cannot see what you do inside other apps.

Do I need an account? Does it cost money?

No. Klarity has no account and no login. It is free, with no subscriptions and no in-app purchases.

How do I connect an app with Shortcuts?

In Klarity, open Settings, then “Shortcuts setup and status”. In the Shortcuts app, create an Automation for when an app is opened, choose to run it immediately, add an “Open URLs” action, and paste the link shown in Klarity for that app (it looks like klarity://pause/<app-id>). Labels in Shortcuts may differ slightly by iOS version. You can turn any automation off in Shortcuts at any time.

Why does it say “Not connected yet” or “Setup not finished”?

The setup screen shows “Not connected yet” until Klarity receives its first pause from a Shortcuts automation. After that first pause, it shows “Connected”. The Today tab shows “Setup not finished” until that same first Shortcuts pause. This does not later check whether an automation has been turned off.

How do I change rules, back them up, or erase my data?

Use the Apps tab to add, remove, or edit an app and its rules (wait, optional daily limit, grace window, optional schedule, and Strict mode). In Settings you can set a daily opens goal, turn on an optional evening check-in (a local notification at 8:00 p.m. on your device clock, if you grant notification permission), and optionally lock rule changes with a 4-digit PIN. “Back up my rules” uses the system share sheet; restore reads the clipboard. “Erase all data” in Settings clears everything Klarity has stored on the device and returns you to the welcome screen. Deleting the app also removes that local data.
